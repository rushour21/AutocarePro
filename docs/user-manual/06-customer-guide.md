# 6. Customer guide

There are two ways for a customer to follow a service: the **private tracking link** (no sign-in) and the **customer portal** (sign in).

## A. The private tracking link

The workshop shares a link such as `/track/<long-code>` for one job. Anyone with the link can see that job, so share it only with the vehicle owner. Job numbers alone do not open a link.

Tabs along the top:
- **Overview**: the car, the current status, service type, booking date, expected delivery, and a progress timeline (Inspection, Approval, In Repair, Quality Check, Ready).
- **Live View**: photos from the workshop and a timeline of the work.
- **Details**: service details.
- **Inspection**: what the technician found.

From the link the customer can also open:
- **Estimate**: review items and totals. **Approve Estimate** (then confirm "Approve" in the dialog) or **Request Changes**.
- **Invoice**: shows the invoice. To pay, **sign in** (paying needs an account).
- **Photos**, **Report**, **Timeline**.
- **Feedback**: opens once the vehicle is ready. Choose 1 to 5 stars, add a comment, and **Submit Feedback**.

When the vehicle is ready the Overview changes to "Your Vehicle is Ready!".

## B. The customer portal

Sign in with your email and password.

### Dashboard
A greeting and shortcuts: **Book Service, My Vehicles, Invoices**. Cards show **Active Service, Pending Approval, Completed**, and **Total Spent**. **Current Service** shows your car's live status.

### My Vehicles
Your cars, with service due dates (for example, "Service due in 12 days"). Add a vehicle with its registration number (format like `MH12AB1234`).

### Bookings
1. Select **Book Service**.
2. **Select Vehicle**, **Choose Service** (General Service, Brake Service, Engine Service, AC Service, Suspension, Diagnostics, Detailing, or Custom Request), pick a **Date and Time**.
3. **Confirm Booking**. The booking is shown as **Requested** until the workshop confirms it.

### Estimates
- Estimates waiting for you show **Awaiting Approval**. Open one to see every line, the GST, and the total.
- **Approve** to let the workshop start, or **Request Changes** to ask for a revision.

### Invoices
1. Open **Invoices**, then **View** on an invoice.
2. For a pending invoice, select **Pay** (UPI in the demo). The invoice becomes **Paid** and you receive a confirmation.
3. **Download** gives a printable copy.

### Service History, Notifications, Share Link, Support
- **Service History**: past jobs and invoices.
- **Notifications**: updates such as "Your vehicle is in Repair", "Estimate awaiting approval", and "Payment received".
- **Share Link**: copy the tracking link for a job to share with family.
- **Support**: contact the workshop.
- **Profile**: your details, payment methods, and security.

### Insurance claims
If your job is an insurance claim, the job page shows an **Insurance claim** card: what the insurer pays and what **you pay**. Review the breakdown and select **Approve** to confirm your share. See [Insurance claims](07-insurance-claims.md).

## Quick answers for customers

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Where is my car? | Open the tracking link or the portal dashboard |
| How do I approve extra work? | Open the estimate and select **Approve Estimate** |
| Do I need an app? | No. The link works in any browser |
| How do I pay? | Sign in, open **Invoices**, and select **Pay** |
