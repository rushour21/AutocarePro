# 5. Technician guide

Technicians see only the jobs assigned to them. The menu has **Dashboard, My Jobs, Inspections, Parts Request, Calendar, Messages, Team, Profile**. On a phone, a bottom bar gives quick access to the main sections.

## Dashboard

A greeting, then cards for **My Jobs** (assigned today), **In Progress**, **Quality Check**, and **Due Today**. **Current Job** shows the job you are working on, with its status and a shortcut to open it.

## My Jobs

- Filters: **All, Assigned to Me, In Progress, On Hold, Completed**.
- Each card shows the job number, car, plate, status, progress bar, and a **Continue** or **Start** button.
- Select a card or **Continue** to open the job.

## Working a job

1. Open the job. At the top you see the job number, car and plate.
2. **Update the status** with the status selector (for example, In Repair to Quality Check). A confirmation message appears ("Status: Quality Check").
3. The page shows the car, **photos** of the work and findings, and the **customer** with call and message buttons.
4. Use the tabs for **Tasks**, **notes**, and **parts**.
5. **Update Progress** opens the job checklist. Tick items as you finish them.
6. When the work is done, move the job to **Quality Check**, then **Ready**.

Tips: keep the status current. Customers and the owner see it live.

## Inspections

Open **Inspections** (or a job's inspection page) to record findings on a car diagram.
1. Choose a view: **3D View**, **Photos**, **Checklist**.
2. Tap a point on the car (front bumper, doors, wheels, and so on).
3. Set the condition: **Good**, **Minor Issue**, **Major Issue**, or **Not Checked**.
4. For a problem, choose the **damage type** (Scratch, Dent, Paint Damage, Cracked, Missing, or Custom) and **severity** (Minor, Major, Critical), and write **notes**.
5. **Add Photo** to attach pictures. Photos support the estimate and protect the workshop in a dispute.

The customer can see the inspection report on their tracking link.

## Parts Request

1. Open **Parts Request**.
2. On **Request Parts**, search the list or pick from **Common Parts** (for example, Brake Pad, Brake Disc, Engine Oil, Air Filter). Each shows current stock.
3. Select **Request**. In the window, choose the **job** (**For job**) and the **quantity**, then **Send request**.
4. Track it under **My Requests**. The status is **Pending**, **Approved**, or **Rejected**. The admin decides in Inventory.

If you need a part that is not listed, tell the admin through **Messages**.

## Calendar, messages, team, profile

- **Calendar** shows your schedule for the week.
- **Messages** has job and team conversations.
- **Team** lists colleagues. **Profile** has your details and preferences.

## Checklist for every job

- [ ] Check the car's condition and record damage before starting.
- [ ] Take photos of anything unusual.
- [ ] Request parts early.
- [ ] Do not start extra work until the estimate is approved.
- [ ] Update the status at every step.
